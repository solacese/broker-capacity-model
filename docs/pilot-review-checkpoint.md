# Codex review of the first pilot implementation

Review performed while Claude was building checkpoint 1 on 2026-10-05. These are review findings, not final current-code assertions; close each with a regression test and documented outcome before full-rate experiments.

1. **Blocker — ignored broker discards.** `BrokerSample.cumulative_counters` was recorded but never evaluated in `classify_stage`. A run with increasing ingress/egress discards could pass. Require relevant mode-specific counters, detect resets at every adjacent sample (including mid-window resets), and classify new unexplained loss. Historical counts alone remain harmless.
2. **Blocker — sample window not enforced.** First/last samples could be outside the measurement window, unordered, or fail to cover it and still pass. Validate timestamps, coverage, sampling gaps, and measurement duration; only derive metrics over covered measurement time. A 60s wiring calibration must not be labeled a full 300s saturation stage.
3. **Blocker — gauges mistaken for counters.** Queue depth/spool/unacked can decrease legitimately. `_queue_growth` used counter-reset logic and summed gauges across queues. Use signed per-queue gauge differences and successive-window slopes; one growing queue must not be hidden by another draining. A transient rise that recovers is distinct from sustained positive drift.
4. **Blocker — offered load conservation.** Requested/scheduled/attempted/successful/accepted counts need nonnegative and ordering validation plus agreement with requested_rate × covered_duration. Zero traffic at nonzero requested rate currently can pass. The attempted counter was collected but not checked. Do not attribute low successful publish rate automatically to a client ceiling: broker backpressure can cause it. Require corroborating generator evidence, otherwise INCONCLUSIVE/unknown mechanism.
5. **Blocker — destination-level conservation.** Aggregate received/expected can hide a starved subscriber, extra receipts, or duplicates. Validate each unique destination count, impossible overdelivery, retransmits and in-flight-window accounting. Require evidence provenance, mode-appropriate publisher ACK semantics (Direct does not give persistent ACKs), and no fabricated Direct accepted-message evidence.
6. **Blocker — contradictory search labels.** Search ignored an UNSUSTAINABLE observation below a later SUSTAINABLE observation. This must stop the monotone search and flag nonstationarity/conflicting evidence; do not silently discard the lower failure. Validate finite positive rates and min/max bounds, including after only-invalid observations. Add stage/retry budget or explicit caller enforcement.
7. **Feature scope.** Current Workload exposes one protocol, while the campaign requires independent ingress/egress protocol; persistent queue type, partition/fanout/ACK settings, intended composition and broker identity/config hashes must be recorded by eventual run manifests. Do not claim that the plan/bracket CLI is already a live benchmark runner.

Required adversarial regression cases: increasing discards with otherwise healthy counts; all-zero publishes at nonzero target; one destination overdelivering while another underdelivers; mid-window counter reset with recovered final value; one queue grows while another drains; falling gauges are not counter resets; out-of-window and unordered samples; fail at 100 then pass at 200; NaN/infinite rates; invalid retry never exceeding maximum_rate.
